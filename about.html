About AMRAVATI360

A Geospatial Intelligence and GeoAI platform mapping every essential facility in Amravati city on one smart, searchable map.

🎯 Project Objective

To design and develop a Web GIS platform that consolidates scattered public-facility information — hospitals, colleges, ATMs, transport, government offices, and more — into a single, live, searchable geospatial interface for the city of Amravati, Maharashtra.

❓ Problem Statement

Citizens of Amravati currently have no single, reliable source to locate essential city facilities. Information is fragmented across government offices, word-of-mouth, and inconsistent online listings. There is no existing platform that combines mapping, spatial analysis, and natural-language search specifically for Amravati.

🧭 Aim

To build a scalable, open-data-driven Geospatial Intelligence system that improves civic accessibility and demonstrates the practical application of GIS + GeoAI concepts learned during the MSc Geoinformatics programme.

Web GIS

🗂️ Data Sources & Tech Stack

  • Data: OpenStreetMap (Overpass API), manually verified locations (Google Maps, 2026-09-03)
  • Mapping: Leaflet.js, Leaflet.markercluster, Leaflet.heat
  • Visualization: Chart.js for analytics dashboard
  • Frontend: HTML, CSS, vanilla JavaScript (no framework dependency)
  • GeoAI logic: Custom JavaScript NLP + spatial distance calculations

🚀 Applications

Urban planning support, tourism assistance, emergency-service discovery, civic transparency, and as a template for similar Smart City GIS platforms in other Tier-2/3 Indian cities.

🔮 Future Scope

  • Integrating a real government open-data feed for civic infrastructure
  • Adding 3D building visualization
  • Connecting a live LLM API for deeper natural-language understanding
  • Crowd-sourced place verification and community reporting
